Step 1 — Your free Snowflake trial account

About 5 minutes

Snowflake trial signup — use this link, not a signup page you find yourself.

Leave the region exactly as the link sets it — Asia Pacific (Tokyo). It looks wrong from Perth. It isn’t. It’s pre-selected so you land in a region with the AI features this workshop needs, and changing it will break the lab.

Choose Enterprise edition when prompted. Then check the activation email, sign in to Snowsight in your browser, and confirm your role shows as ACCOUNTADMIN in the top right — you’ll be creating databases, search services and agents today, and you need the permissions to do it.

Please use a fresh trial account rather than your organisation’s existing Snowflake account. You’ll be creating and deleting things freely all morning, and on a trial you can do that without a second thought.

Step 2 — CoCo Desktop

About 10 minutes

CoCo is Snowflake’s AI assistant for building things on Snowflake — it’s what you’ll be driving all morning.

1. Download it for macOS or Windows: CoCo Desktop downloads.

On Windows you’ll be offered two options — pick the User installer. It installs for you only and needs no administrator rights, so a laptop managed by your IT team is not a blocker.

2. Open it and connect to the trial account you just created. It walks you through this on first launch.

The command-line version of CoCo isn’t needed today. We’re working from the desktop app.

The 30-second test

In the CoCo Desktop chat box, type:

What Snowflake account, user and role am I connected as?

If it answers with your trial account and ACCOUNTADMIN, you’re set. That’s everything.

If you see this, stop and flag it — don’t try to fix it:

Access denied for trial accounts

It means AI features need switching on for your account. It’s a known thing, it is not something you’ve done wrong, and it takes us a minute to sort. Just show one of us the screen.
