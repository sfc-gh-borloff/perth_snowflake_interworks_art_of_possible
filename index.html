Art of the Possible

A hands-on morning, not a demo you watch. You’ll build and deploy a working AI agent over sample sales data by describing what you want in plain English — then hand it to a business user and build something of your own.

Welcome

Everything you need for the morning lives on this page. You will be driving the tools yourself — typing what you want in plain English and watching it get built — rather than listening to someone describe it. Led from the front, step by step, with people circulating to help.

You do not need to be an engineer, and you do not need to bring any data. The sample company is invented and the whole dataset gets generated on the day. The point of the morning is to find out what is genuinely possible now, on your own data problems, with your own hands on the keyboard.

What you’ll have built by lunchtime

Six things, all of them yours to keep.

A loaded dataset

200 deals and 150 call transcripts, generated from a plain-English description.

Searchable transcripts

Semantic and keyword search over unstructured text, with no pipeline to build.

A semantic view

Your business definitions written down, so plain-English questions become accurate SQL.

A deployed agent

One agent that routes across structured and unstructured data, with citations.

The same agent in CoWork

Used by a business person in a chat interface. No code, nothing redeployed.

Something of your own

Whatever you choose to build in the final session, running in your own account.

All of it stays in your trial account for 30 days — so you can show a colleague on Friday, or pick it apart again next week.

Arrived and not set up yet?

No problem at all, and you won’t be the only one. There are only two things. Start here — about 15 minutes and grab any of the Snowflake or InterWorks team in the room.
